Objective 1 · Requirements and networking architecture

Analyze personal and organizational requirements and design an appropriate networking architecture.

My work and contribution

I developed a network architecture proposal for a coursework scenario involving offices in New York, London, and Tokyo. I compared MPLS with SD-WAN and selected an SD-WAN-based hybrid approach based on cost and scalability considerations. I described core, distribution, and access functions, with proposed redundant edge routers and uplinks to address availability requirements.

For a healthcare infrastructure scenario, I mapped storage, email, web hosting, database, and authentication needs to a combination of AWS services and an on-premises Active Directory server. My contribution was to connect each organizational requirement to an infrastructure choice and explain the intended security, redundancy, and growth considerations. These proposals demonstrate requirements analysis and architecture design across both multi-site networking and hybrid server infrastructure.

Objective 3 · Misconfigurations and performance solutions

Identify networking misconfigurations and determine solutions to achieve optimal performance.

My work and contribution

I evaluated a segment of two telecommunications networks being combined after an acquisition. I identified reliance on wireless backhaul and an expensive leased uplink site as constraints on the proposed combined network. I then developed an optimization proposal that consolidates coverage, moves customers to selected remaining sites, and adds fiber connections to Dewey and North Cherry.

My contribution was to analyze the existing layout, document an alternative design, and explain the expected performance and operating-cost benefits. I also considered migration risks, construction costs, and the need to verify that the remaining sites could support the additional customers. This work demonstrates the performance-analysis and solution-design portions of the objective through a documented network optimization proposal.

Objective 5 · Traditional networking, IoT and mobile devices

Create networking solutions that incorporate traditional networking, IoT, and mobile devices.

My work and contribution

I developed a tower-mounted distribution project to address cabling and expansion needs at fixed-wireless sites. I completed two field installations: the first prototype provided fiber distribution, and the second added power distribution. My contribution was to develop and install the physical distribution approach and refine it between iterations.

I then designed a monitoring extension to connect the physical infrastructure to remote fault reporting. The earlier concept proposed a Raspberry Pi and LTE connection for email and SMS alerts. My later prototype plan uses a 12-volt Arduino/LoRaWAN approach with backup power and a separately powered gateway. I documented the design progression in presentations and project briefs. The work demonstrates completed physical-network prototyping and a proposed IoT monitoring architecture, with mobile notification included in the earlier design.

Objective 6 · Standards-based network security

Demonstrate security principles within networking solutions based on industry standards.

My work and contribution

I authored a cybersecurity implementation plan that used NIST guidance to organize proposed networking security improvements for a communications provider. I included BGP route-origin validation using RPKI in the proposed routing-security approach and used the Cybersecurity Framework functions described in the paper to structure risk management, protection, detection, response, and recovery planning.

In a separate AWS lab, I created security-group rules that distinguish public web access from restricted administrative access. My contribution was to explain standards-informed security choices in the plan and configure a concrete network access-control example in the lab. These artifacts demonstrate security planning based on named industry guidance alongside practical firewall-rule configuration.
