Objective 1 · Network infrastructure design communications document

Create a network infrastructure design communications document that includes identified hardware components, connections to the outside world, identified physical layer connectivity (media) and addressing, including operational and security components in the design.

My work and contribution

I created network design documentation at both office and service-provider scale. My medical-office diagram identifies a 1 Gbps FTTP internet connection, a Netgate security gateway, 10 Gbps fiber and 2.5 GbE connections, and VLANs 10 and 20. I used the diagram to communicate how the external connection, internal equipment, and network segmentation fit together.

For my logical and physical topology project, I developed a rural fiber-network redesign proposal using ADTRAN access equipment and Cisco routing and switching equipment. I documented a proposed 10 Gbps ring, backbone fiber, PON distribution, and ring protection. My contribution was to translate connectivity and capacity needs into diagrams and a written explanation of the hardware and media choices. Together, these artifacts demonstrate my ability to communicate infrastructure design, including operational resilience and security segmentation.

Objective 2 · Install, configure and test security tools

Install, configure and test security hardware and software tools with supporting documentation such as port scanners, vulnerability detection systems, intrusion detection systems, firewalls, system hardening, anti-virus tools, patch management, auditing and assessment.

My work and contribution

In an Ubuntu EC2 lab, I checked UFW through the package manager, allowed SSH before enabling the firewall, and verified its active status and displayed rules. UFW was already installed, so my work focused on configuration and verification. I documented the sequence with screenshots showing the SSH rule, firewall activation, and status output.

In a separate AWS lab, I created a security group with inbound rules for HTTP on port 80 and HTTPS on port 443 from any IPv4 address, while restricting SSH on port 22 to a specified source. My contribution was to configure and document different access rules for public services and remote administration. These labs demonstrate host and cloud firewall configuration, supported by a UFW status check and an AWS rule-creation record.

Objective 3 · Automate a security-related process

Construct, implement and document a script or a program to automate a security-related process or other tasks such as installation, administration, management, mapping resources, logon scripts, patch management, updates, auditing, analysis and assessment.

My work and contribution

For my security scripting project, I combined service scanning, vulnerability lookups, and report generation in a Bash workflow. The submitted script invokes Nmap service detection and vulnerability scripts, extracts open-service information, checks selected service versions, and queries the National Vulnerability Database. It organizes the results and remediation recommendations into a dated report. The saved report records detected services and labels NVD results as potential matches requiring validation.

I also configured an Ansible inventory and adapted an update-and-upgrade playbook for a remote Ubuntu instance. After troubleshooting the connection, I added remote_user: ubuntu to the supplied playbook and documented a successful run with one changed task and no failures. A subsequent package check listed no available upgrades. These projects demonstrate my contribution to automating security assessment and patch management, with source code, report output, and a documented remote automation result.

Objective 4 · Policy or procedure

I authored a disaster-recovery plan comparing cloud, on-premises, and hybrid recovery approaches. I organized the plan around risk assessment, business impact, recovery time and recovery point objectives, backup strategy, replication, and planned validation. My contribution was to connect technical recovery options to the business consequences of downtime and data loss. These documents demonstrate my ability to create recovery and continuity planning material, explain the proposed procedures, and identify how their effectiveness should be evaluated.

Objective 5 · Legal and ethical information-security practices

Develop a research report or implementation plan concerning legal and ethical best practices and mandated requirements that pertain to information security.

My work and contribution

I wrote a cybersecurity implementation plan for a communications-service-provider scenario that considered customer privacy, information handling, and compliance risks alongside technical controls. The plan discusses GDPR and CCPA and uses NIST guidance to organize proposed improvements. I included employee training and proper handling of customer information as part of the proposed security approach.

I also researched the GRID Act proposal and analyzed its approach to electric-grid protection, emergency response, and coordination among public and private organizations. My contribution was to examine the relationship between security decisions, proposed legal requirements, and responsibility to customers and the public. These artifacts demonstrate research and planning concerning the legal and ethical dimensions of information security.

Objective 6 · Research, test and evaluate security threats and mitigations

Research, document, test and evaluate several current industry information security-based threats, risks, malicious activities, covert methodology, encryption technologies, mitigation techniques or unconventional tactics to prevent loss of sensitive information and data confidentiality, integrity and availability.

My work and contribution

I researched phishing and social engineering in a telecommunications context, including targeted phishing and pretexting. My report evaluates how these techniques can expose customer information, enable unauthorized access, and disrupt operations. I proposed mitigations including awareness training, multifactor authentication, email filtering, incident response planning, and monitoring.

My scanner project adds a technical assessment artifact to that research. The saved report records open services, no positive NSE vulnerability findings, and a potential NVD keyword match for further investigation. I organized the assessment workflow to separate detected services from potential vulnerabilities and to include recommendations for validation and remediation. Together, the research report and scanner package demonstrate threat analysis, a recorded service scan, and documented mitigation recommendations.
