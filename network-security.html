Objective 3

Construct, implement and document a script or a program to automate a security-related process or other tasks such as installation, administration, management, mapping resources, logon scripts, patch management, updates, auditing, analysis and assessment.

Project 1 · NTS370 Vulnerability Scanner

I built a script that brings several parts of a security assessment into one repeatable process. It runs Nmap to identify services, looks up potential vulnerabilities in the National Vulnerability Database, and writes the results and remediation suggestions to a report. The dated scan report shows how the script turns service discovery into a list of findings I can follow up on. I treat database matches as leads and check them against the actual service version and configuration before calling them confirmed vulnerabilities.

Evidence · Dated scan report, lines 5–50, covering discovered services, potential matches, and remediation suggestions. View the scanner repository.

Project 2 · NTW216 Remote Patch Management with Ansible

I used an Ansible playbook to automate package updates on a remote Linux host. The playbook put the update steps into a repeatable procedure that I could run without signing in and entering each command manually. The run recorded a change with zero failed or unreachable hosts, and my follow-up check showed no remaining upgradeable packages. Together, those results show both the patching action and how I verified its outcome.

Evidence · Ansible recap: ok=2, changed=1, unreachable=0, failed=0. A subsequent package check listed no upgradeable packages.

Objective 4

Create a policy or procedure that addresses events such as: a disaster recovery plan, a business continuity plan, an incident response policy, an acceptable usage document, an information security policy, a physical security policy, assessments or troubleshooting procedures.

Project 1 · NTS336 AWS Disaster Recovery Plan

I developed a disaster recovery plan that uses recovery time and recovery point objectives to guide decisions about backups and restoration. The recovery time objective helps determine how quickly a service needs to return, while the recovery point objective helps determine how much data loss the organization can tolerate. I used those considerations to explain why backup and recovery choices need to reflect the needs of the service, and I included a plan for validating that the recovery process could meet those needs.

Evidence · Disaster recovery plan, pp. 3–6, covering backup planning, AWS recovery, and planned validation.

Project 2 · NTW216 Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Strategy

I developed a continuity strategy that connects backup, replication, and recovery to the goal of keeping an organization operating through a disruption. I explained how backups provide a point to restore from and how replication can reduce the time needed to bring services back. That distinction gives the organization a basis for deciding what needs to be protected ahead of time and what needs to happen when a service goes down.

Evidence · Continuity strategy, pp. 2–4, covering proposed AWS backup, replication, and recovery measures.
